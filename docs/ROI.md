# ROI (scenario, not a claim)

Formula: `eligible cases/month × minutes saved/case ÷ 60 × loaded hourly cost`.

## Editable inputs

| Input | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eligible cases/month | 300 | 600 | 1,200 |
| Minutes saved/case | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| Loaded hourly cost ($) | 60 | 90 | 120 |

## Scenario range

- Low: 300 × 10/60 × 60 = **$3,000/mo** gross labor capacity value.
- Mid: 600 × 15/60 × 90 = **$13,500/mo**.
- High: 1,200 × 20/60 × 120 = **$48,000/mo**.

Cash savings require changed staffing or avoided hiring; do not subtract an
invented tool cost. First confirm with fal: eligible investigation volume,
handling time, and review effort. fal's actual savings are unknown.
